## Investing

- **Great businesses over cigar butts:** "I saw immediately that Graham was wrong." Real money is in companies that "carried you up, and up." — CNBC 2023
- **Patience and few bets:** "You make your money by the waiting." Life gives "one, or two, or three" fair allotments — DJCO 2016
- **Bet heavily on edge:** "When you know you have an edge, you should bet heavily." — Acquired 2023; "Maybe five, six times in a lifetime" for Costco-scale ideas — same
- **Harder now:** "Why should it be easy?" Making money should be hard — Acquired 2023
- **Circle of competence:** Stay out of others' strong suits; don't predict Silicon Valley winners — CNBC 2023; DJCO 2016
- **Too hard pile:** Most problems go there; only easy decisions get prompt action — DJCO 2019–2022
- **Value never obsolete:** "The idea of getting more value than you pay for... is never going to be obsolete." — DJCO 2022
- **Efficient markets ignored:** Berkshire succeeded "without paying one ounce of attention to the efficient market theory in its hard form." — UCSB 2003
- **Gambling parlor markets:** Tax short-term gains; separate gambling from capital formation — Acquired 2023; DJCO 2022
- **See's / brand pricing:** 10% annual raises, "nobody cared" — Acquired 2023
